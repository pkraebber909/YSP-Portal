# Logo batches 12 and 13

The archives contain 14 confirmed images. Eleven are club marks: nine fill
empty CRM logo slots, and two replace reviewed existing marks. The other three
are league marks and have no club assignment.

The supplied Port Chester SC image is a cleaner transparent copy of the same
Port Chester 2007 ship crest already on the `portchester` record. The SC Gjøa
image is the club's documented 2025 ship redesign, corroborated by the
designer's case study. Both replacements are guarded by the exact previous
seed path, so a later staff-selected logo is preserved. The SC Gjøa source is
an opaque navy JPEG; its original background has been retained.

Big Apple Youth Soccer League, Capital District Youth Soccer League, and
Hudson Valley Youth Soccer League are identified as **leagues** in the Batch 13
manifest. Their existing directory rows are typed as clubs in the prototype.
Their marks were therefore held out of the club logo map pending a separate
league classification. No club records were created or merged.

The two ZIPs also contain 15 review-list entries without packaged images;
those remain unchanged. The Farmingdale and Franklin Square Raiders files are
small originals suitable only for small on-screen display. All added files
retain their original bytes, proportions, backgrounds and transparency.

See [`logo-reconciliation-batches-12-13.json`](logo-reconciliation-batches-12-13.json)
for source URLs, hashes, original filenames, exact decisions, stable IDs, and
the full review lists. No partnership, rep ownership, contact, note, source,
or next-action data was changed.
